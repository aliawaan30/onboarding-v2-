# Filming notes: AI Websites module (builder-only, never student-facing)
_Rewritten 2026-08-26 for the A-F structure. Read before recording. One build-through =
the Looms + every screenshot harvested._

## The one decision still open: THE PRICE STORY
The call hook says "I'm not going to charge you thousands like the agencies." The real
number is unsettled: $500? $1200? $2000? Every $[price] placeholder on pages A, C, E and F
waits on this. Pick one number and say it identically everywhere.

## Page A: Why every business needs a website (narrated)
- Intro angle: open with the finished demo site on screen ("this took an afternoon, watch
  how"), then the invisible-business story.
- Talking points to elaborate on camera: the searcher picks someone else and the business
  never knows; agencies charge thousands and take six weeks; the customer-pain opener (you
  are about to make them FEEL it); the knee-surgeon niche beat.
- Fill the demo-site frame: screenshot of your best spec site.

## Page B: Build your Website Lead Finder
- This is the prompt-heavy Loom. Follow the page live, paste by paste.
- The blueprint is now a downloadable SKILL (website-lead-finder). On camera: download it,
  upload it, show the agent playing the blueprint back.
- Statuses are the pipeline now: Found > Called > Meeting Booked > Site Built > Claimed >
  Live (+ Dead). Show the coloured dropdown once built.
- Screenshots to harvest: Google Cloud key screen (blur your key), the first search
  results, the Leads board with pipeline colours.
- Replace draft prompts with your proven sequence anywhere the build diverges.

## Page C: Call them
- The script is the Dave framework adapted (mystery-shopper opener). Explain the origin on
  camera if you like: you open as a customer, they feel the missed booking live.
- Founder-story slot: the script has a one-line "why I do this" beat. Give them YOUR line
  as the example, and the page has their co-founder write theirs.
- Demo the voice-mode roleplay on camera. It is the moment of the page.
- Objection beats: too good to be true / I already have Facebook / no time.
- Booking flow: get their email on the call, co-founder sends the calendar invite + Meet
  link before you hang up.

## Page D: Build their website
- The archetype skill (website-archetypes) is YOUR IP. The shipped file is a v1 draft;
  your own version supersedes it, swap the file before filming if yours is ready.
- On camera: explain the five archetypes in one breath each, then let the one-shot build
  run and react to it honestly.
- The practice note: while learning they can build freely (roughly a credit a site). Say
  the economics on camera, keep numbers off the page.
- Portfolio move: show adding 2-3 spec sites to your own M2 proof page.
- Screenshots: the archetype pick moment, the one-shot site fresh out, before/after of one
  refinement.

## Page E: The sales call
- Frame: the meeting was booked on Page C, the site was built on Page D, this is the
  screen share.
- The honest line to model: "built this from your public info in an afternoon, everything
  is customisable."
- The live customization loop is the wow: get a feature request, build it while they
  watch. Rehearse one on camera.

## Page F: Publish it and make it theirs
- Publish + domain connect: your live walkthrough IS the lesson, capture every screen
  (registrar record, the propagation wait line, the padlock check on mobile data).
- Registrar choice still open (which one the course recommends).
- Invoice beat: Revolut flow, same as walkthroughs.
- End on the congratulations energy: their business is finally on the map.

## Screenshot slots placed in the pages
B: key screen, first results, pipeline board. D: one-shot site. E: none required. F:
registrar record screen, padlock on phone. Fill during your build-through.
