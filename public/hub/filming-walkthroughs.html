# Filming notes: AI Walkthroughs module (builder-only, never student-facing)

## Page A: Sell the video before you make it (narrated page)
- INTRO ANGLE: open by SHOWING the end result. "By the end of this module you'll have the
  spine of your AI walkthrough video service." Play a finished walkthrough in the first
  30 seconds, then talk over the page.
- The three demo video frames are placeholders. Drop in 3 real demo walkthroughs (Blake
  to supply files/links; replace the placeholder divs with embeds).
- THE MATE'S-INTEL TALKING BLOCK (say this on camera, it is not written on the page):
  agencies pay videographer teams $300 to $500 per property walkthrough. Most listings
  never get one because the VENDOR (the homeowner) refuses the cost: $500 out of pocket
  before the sale, even though video lifts results. At $150 to $200, the maths flips:
  the agent can put it on the vendor's bill without a fight, it becomes an easy add-on
  sale for the agent, and for a student it is the perfect first-client play. Cheap,
  same-day, no-brainer.
- Elaborate on: why call-first matters (credits cost money, interest before generation).

## Page B: Build your Studio (18 steps)
- Step 1 is now the BLUEPRINT SKILL install: explain why the whole app lives in a skill
  (the agent permanently knows the design, every build comes out identical, rebuilds are
  trivial). Wait for the "ready to get started" reply on camera.
- Record one real build-through: paste each piece, show the agent building, screenshot
  as you go.
- SCREENSHOT FRAMES placed at: step 2 (your apps screen with the blank app), step 6
  (the empty pipeline dashboard), step 10 (the photo strip), step 12 (a storyboard card).
- Step 16 checkpoint: walk the status diagram out loud, "you only flip two by hand."
- VERIFY DURING YOUR BUILD: the pieces produce YOUR clean app look; patch any visual gap
  back into the skill file and the piece prompts afterwards.

## Page C: Fill your pipeline (6 steps)
- Show the real hunt live: open realestate.com.au, spot a photo-only listing, screenshot
  it, send it to the co-founder, watch the card land on the board. That moment sells the
  whole system.
- Elaborate on: what makes a good target listing (nice photos, mid-to-high price, active
  agent).

## Page D: Call them (12 steps)
- THE SCRIPT IS A STAND-IN. Blake's final call wording still to come (marked with an HTML
  comment in the page). The trick line is deliberate and stays: "I've actually created a
  couple of videos for listings just like yours" gets the interest, the video gets built
  AFTER they're keen, same day. Explain the ethics of it your way on camera: you have
  made videos like theirs (the practice one), and theirs exists within hours of the yes.
- NEW STEP 4: voice-mode roleplay. DEMO THIS ON CAMERA, it is the coolest moment on the
  page: your co-founder plays a skeptical agent out loud.
- On a yes: email + 15 minute Google Meet booked for tomorrow. That Meet is where the
  video gets SHOWN (never sent), page G explains why.
- Skill installs: show the download chip, the upload, and mention the copy-the-full-
  message path for phone users.

## Page E: Your first video (6 steps)
- ELABORATE (Blake's note): how to ideate the house's WALKING ORDER: front, entry,
  hallway, living, kitchen, bedrooms, backyard. Think "how would a buyer walk it".
- Show writing 2-3 scene descriptions on camera, then Generate All and the prompts
  appearing under the cards.
- SCREENSHOT FRAMES: step 2 (the storyboard), step 5 (the Generate All Prompts button,
  arrow on the button).

## Page F: Generate and stitch (7 steps)
- Show one full clip generation on Envato end to end (upload pair, paste prompt, preset,
  5s, 16:9, render, download).
- SCREENSHOT FRAMES: step 3 (Envato's generator mid-render), step 6 (the stitch box with
  the Copy button, arrow on it).
- The stitch: send the message, cut to the finished video landing. Big moment, let it
  breathe.
- OPEN: insert Blake's Envato affiliate link where the page says elements.envato.com.
- NOTE: the Envato signup step may move to the onboarding call later, leave as-is for now.
- VERIFY on a fresh account: the first stitch run (ffmpeg in the agent sandbox).

## Page G: Deliver, charge, repeat (8 steps)
- Delivery is now Meet-first: share screen, play the video, never send the file before
  the invoice. Say the why with conviction: a sent file can be used without paying.
- Price is live on the pages: $150 to $200 a video, small unit low end, big family home
  high end.
- The bundle step is now a co-founder prompt (it builds the offer); read the retainer
  beat with full conviction, it is the recurring-money step.
- The vertical-reel upsell is PARKED (not in the course right now).
