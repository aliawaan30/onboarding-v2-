Module 2 · Build your business · Page B

Build your website

By the end of this page you have a one page website in your own colours, pointed straight at the kind of business you picked.

You send the messages. Your co-founder does every bit of the building.

  1. 1

    Step 01Create The Empty App

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Open Base44, start a new app, and paste this in as the very first message.

    Create an app called "[YOUR BUSINESS NAME]". Do not build anything inside it. Do not add any pages, tables or features. Just create it with that name and tell me when it is done.
    You should see: your business name in your Base44 apps list, and nothing inside it when you open it. That is exactly right.

    This is the rule for every app in this course. You make the empty app, your co-founder builds inside it.

  2. 2

    Step 02Tell Your Co-Founder The App Exists

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    I've created an empty app called [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]. That's where my website goes. I'll feed you one piece at a time. Reply "ready to build" once you've got it.
  3. 3

    Step 03Build The Shape Of The Site

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    One message builds the first design. The following steps refine it without rebuilding the layout.

    Build the first version of my own agency website inside my [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] app. Read my installed my-agency-site skill first and follow its layout, visual and copy rules. Use my saved service, niche, area and brand. Build the complete visual composition with real draft copy and the available relevant demonstration. Keep the desktop offer beside the example, then stack them clearly on a phone. No empty proof slots or invented clients. We refine the words in the following steps. Keep the same design as we go. If an example or business detail is genuinely missing, tell me what is needed instead of inventing it. No prices anywhere.
    You should see: a designed page with your offer and a relevant example near the top, clear sections below and a layout that works on a phone. Any missing assets should be explained.
  4. 4

    Step 04Write Your Headline And Your Offer

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    This is the most important thing on the site. It is the bit that decides whether somebody keeps reading or closes the tab.

    Write the big headline at the top of my page, the line under it, and my offer line. Follow my-agency-site. Say clearly what service I sell, what the first step is, and any delivery time I have actually confirmed. No clever wordplay, no welcome message, no "your partner in digital growth". Use my niche, my area and my service from memory. Make the audience clear in the headline or a short line above it. Do not force my city and niche into a clumsy headline. The offer line makes saying yes the easy thing to do: what they get, what the first step is, and how little they have to do to take it. Leave builder and tool names out. Describe an AI receptionist or AI-generated walkthrough honestly when that is the service. No prices anywhere on my site, ever. Give me 3 headlines and 3 offer lines, let me pick one of each, then put my picks on the page.
    You should see: a clear service headline with the intended customer visible nearby, and an offer line a busy owner could act on.

    Read your headline to somebody who does not work in tech. If they ask "so what do you actually do?", it failed. Ask for three more.

  5. 5

    Step 05Fill In What You Do

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Fill in the three items in my "What we do" section. Use my service and my niche from memory, and write only about the service I actually sell. Do not mention the other two. Each one is a short heading and two lines. Each one names a real problem a business like theirs has, then the fix. Plain words. No jargon, no buzzwords, no "leverage" or "solutions" or "streamline". No prices.
    You should see: three items, each naming a problem a business owner would recognise as theirs.
  6. 6

    Step 06Fill In How It Works

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Fill in my "How it works" section with three numbered steps, from the customer's point of view, not mine. What happens when they contact me, what happens next, and what they end up with. One line each. Include roughly how long it takes. No prices.
    You should see: three short steps that a stranger could read and know exactly what working with you feels like.
  7. 7

    Step 07Write The About Section

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Nobody hires you because of your life story. They hire you because of what you do for people like them.

    Write my About section using my real identity and how working directly with me works. One short paragraph, three sentences at most. Use I if I am a solo operator, and use my niche and area where relevant. No story about how I started, no years of experience, no clients, no numbers, nothing invented. If you are not sure of a fact about me, leave it out.
    You should see: a short, factual introduction to the person behind the business and how to work with them.
  8. 8

    Step 08Set Up Your Proof Section Honestly

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    You have no clients yet. That is completely normal and you are not going to pretend otherwise.

    Refine the Example section already built under my-agency-site. Show one relevant available demonstration clearly, with a short explanation of what it shows. Label practice work as a Concept project and supplied work as a Course demonstration. Do not claim either is client work. No empty proof slots, invented clients, reviews, badges or results. If no usable example is available, tell me what is missing and help me prepare it using the skill's guidance.

    A fictional concept must be labelled clearly. Replace it with genuine work as you complete jobs. Never present a concept as a client commission.

  9. 9

    Step 09Find Out What They Will Push Back On

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Every kind of business has the same few reasons for saying no. If your page answers them before they say them, you have done half the selling before you ever speak.

    Research the biggest objections a business owner in my niche has about paying for my service. Use my niche and my service from memory. Give me the 5 that come up most. For each one: the objection in the words an owner would actually use, and the honest answer to it. Don't make up statistics or studies. If you are not sure about something, say so.
    You should see: five objections that sound like things a real owner would say out loud.
  10. 10

    Step 10Answer Those Objections On The Page

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Put those 5 objections and answers into the Questions section near the bottom of my page. Write each question the way an owner would ask it, and keep each answer to two lines. Straight answers, no waffle, no prices.

    At least one answer should be slightly uncomfortable to publish. That is the one doing the most work.

  11. 11

    Step 11Put Your Brand On The Whole Page

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Go over the whole page and make my brand consistent. My logo or my business name in the top left of the header, my brand colours on every button, my heading font on every heading, my body font on every paragraph. Use one consistent primary button style and quieter example links. Preserve the layout and responsive sizing from my-agency-site. Nothing left in the default Base44 styling. Also check every header link scrolls to the right section.
    You should see: you can scroll the whole page and nothing jumps out as the wrong colour or the wrong font, and every link takes you to the right place.
  12. 12

    Step 12Make It Perfect On A Phone

    Finish the step above to unlock this one.

    Most people who look you up will be on a phone. So the site gets fixed for a phone before you even look at it.

    Go over my whole page and make it perfect on a phone before I look at it. Check every heading, paragraph, image, form and button at a phone width of 390 pixels. Nothing overlapping, nothing cut off the side, nothing too small to read, buttons big enough to tap with a thumb, and the header links still scroll to the right section. Fix anything that is wrong, then tell me what you changed.

    Now open your site on your actual phone and scroll the whole thing. If something still looks wrong, send this.

    I'm looking at my site on my phone and [SAY EXACTLY WHAT LOOKS WRONG AND WHERE]. Fix that, then go back over the rest of the page at phone width and check nothing else broke.

Page complete

Your site exists, and it is written for one kind of customer.
