Your AI Goes Out and Finds the Customers

You are about to build an app that goes and finds leads for you on autopilot, every single night, while you sleep.

Follow the steps and by the end you will have that app running in your own area, filling a list of businesses you can call in the morning.

How This Used to Work

Finding People to Sell To Was the Hard Part

Up until now this is what everyone did. Sit up at eleven at night scrolling Google Maps, clicking business after business, copying names and phone numbers into a spreadsheet, then checking each one by hand to see if they even had the problem you fix.

Hours of it, every week, before you have spoken to a single person. It is the most finicky part of selling any agency service, and it is where most people stop.

How It Works Now

Your Co-Founder Does All of It, Overnight

Your app searches the local businesses in your area and keeps only the ones that meet the exact requirement for the service you are selling.

You set it to run automatically at 2:00 AM every single night. It goes out to Google Maps, finds the businesses, saves them into your own list, and writes a short summary of each one next to their name.

You wake up to a list of people every single morning. Call them, build your service, sell it. Your part is the call and the build, and your co-founder helps you with both of those too.

That is hours a day of finding leads, completely automated, by an agent you own.

Your whole agency ends up running out of one app that you built and your co-founder runs.

The Three Services

How It Finds the Leads for Each One

1. Businesses with no website

You find a local business that has no website, you build them one with AI, and you sell it to them.

Your app asks Google for every business of your kind in your area, say every plumber in your town. Google sends back forty or fifty of them with their phone number, address and reviews. Your app then throws away every single one that already has a website. What is left is a list of real businesses near you with no website at all. Those are the people you call.

2. Businesses missing calls

You find a local business that cannot answer all its calls, and has nobody answering the phone at all after it shuts, and you build them an AI receptionist.

Your app asks Google the same way, keeps every business that has a phone number, then reads their opening hours and works out what time they close. A business that shuts at five is losing calls every evening and never finds out. Those are the people you call, and you can ring the number yourself after hours and hear it ring out.

3. Real estate listings with no video

You find a real estate agent putting properties on the market with photos and no video, and you build them a walkthrough video with AI. Some of them have no video at all and want one. Some are already paying a lot for video and would rather pay you less.

Your app asks Google for every real estate agency in your area and saves them all. Then you open the agency's own website, or a listing site, and look at the properties they have up right now. The ones with photos and nothing else are the ones to call about.

All three finders live in the same app, and you build them in the pages after this one.
