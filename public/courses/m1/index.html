What An Agency Is

You've already seen a hundred agencies online, even if nobody called them that.

A web design agency builds websites. An ads agency runs their Facebook and Google ads. A social media agency posts for them. A video editing agency cuts their videos.

Every one of those is a job the business could do itself. It's busy, so it pays someone else instead.

A business pays someone else for one reason. The job needs doing, and they don't want to do it themselves.

One service the single job you do for people
Clients businesses that pay you to do it
The work you actually deliver the thing

That's it. That's an agency.

Now the important bit

What An AI Agency Is

An AI agency is delivering an AI service to a business. That's it.

A business wants a website. Normally that's three people, six weeks and a few thousand dollars.

You sit down with your AI co-founder and it builds the same website in an afternoon.

They pay you what they'd pay anyone. The difference is what it cost you.

And then there's the part nobody tells you.

You're not just using AI to work faster. You're building one agent that runs the whole agency: it finds the clients, builds the service, and keeps track of every job you deliver. It lives on an app you build with it.

An agency that runs on AI, on its own app. That's what you're building here.

No staff. No office. No code. No experience.

The menu

The Three Services

AI Receptionists

I find a business that misses calls, and I build them an AI receptionist.

A plumber can't answer while he's under a sink. A hairdresser can't answer halfway through a haircut. After six, nobody answers at all. The caller rings the next name on the list.

Your AI picks up instead. Depending on the business it books the job into their calendar, or it takes their details and sends them to the owner.

$1,000 to $3,000 to set it up, then $500 a month. This one pays you every month, not once.

AI Websites

I find a business with no website, I make them one with AI, and I sell it to them.

Someone hears about a good local business, looks it up, and finds nothing. So they go with the one that has a website.

You build them a real one with AI, then show it to them finished, before they've paid you anything.

$500 to $5,000 a site, depending on the business and the size of the build.

AI Property Walkthroughs

I find a real estate agent posting listings with no video, and I make them the video.

Agents already pay hundreds of dollars for a video of every property they list. It's a normal line in their budget.

You drop the photos already on the listing into an app you'll build, and a finished walkthrough comes out the same day, for half what they pay now.

$150 to $200 a video, and agents list new properties constantly, so it repeats.

Those are ranges, not fixed prices. Landing the Client teaches you how to price it for each client.

The psychology

Why People Actually Buy

Nobody buys AI.

They buy the missed call that got answered. The booking that came in through the website. The house that sold faster.

And here's the part most people selling anything never learn:

People decide with emotion, then justify with logic.

The strongest emotion in business is the fear of losing what's already theirs.

It's called loss aversion. Losing $100 hurts about twice as much as winning $100 feels good.

So you never sell "get more". You show them what they're already losing:

Missed calls mean jobs are leaking to competitors right now.

No website means customers are picking someone else right now.

No listing video means their properties sit while others move, and they're overpaying for video that does.

Point at the leak, and the emotion does the selling. The logic just signs off.

And people don't buy promises. They buy proof.

So later in this course, you'll build the thing first.

The website exists before the phone call.

The demo receptionist exists before the meeting.

The walkthrough exists before the offer.

Last thing

You don't have to guess. Your co-founder knows all three and it knows you, so it helps you choose.
