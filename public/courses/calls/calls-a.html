The whole business comes down to one conversation

You built the thing in the last module. This module is where you learn to get the meeting, and then get the yes, and your co-founder practises both of them with you out loud before either one is real.

That one conversation happens in two parts. The call that gets you the meeting, and the meeting that gets you the money. Everything either side of those two is already built.

By the end of this module you will have a call script in your own words, you will have practised it out loud until it stops feeling like a script, you will have rung real businesses, and you will know exactly what to say when somebody asks the price.

Page A: Before You Ring Anyone

Nothing to build here. Read it once, then go and practise on the next page.

What this actually is

You Are Not Cold Calling

You are ringing someone about a specific thing you can see is missing from their business.

Cold calling is ringing a stranger to sell them something they have never thought about.

That is not this.

You have their business on your screen. You can see they have no website, or that nobody answered at eight last night, or that their listing has photos and no video. You are ringing about that one thing.

And whichever service you picked, the thing already exists. You built it before you picked up the phone.

"I already made you one. Do you want to see it?"

Nobody says no to that. It costs them nothing to look. That is a completely different phone call to anything a salesperson has ever rung them about.

The one rule

Get The Meeting, Do Not Sell On The First Call

The first call has one job. Book a time to show them.

You are not explaining how it works. You are not quoting. You are not answering how much is it.

If they ask the price in the first minute, you say this:

"Give me two minutes to understand what you actually need, or I will quote you for the wrong thing."

Nobody has ever been offended by that sentence.

The bit nobody tells beginners

Most People Will Not Answer

Here is what a normal day looks like, so you do not think you are failing when you are not.

You ring ten businesses. Six do not pick up. Two are busy and tell you so. One listens and says no. One says yes, send it over.

That is a good day.

The people who quit at this stage quit because they thought two nos in a row meant it does not work. It does not mean that. It means you are two calls closer.

The only number that matters this week is how many times you dialled.

One more thing

You Will Be Nervous, And That Is Fine

Everybody is. The first call is the worst one you will ever make, and it is over in ninety seconds.

So on the next page you write the script with your co-founder, and then you make that first call to it instead of to a real person, three times, until the words come out without you thinking about them.

Then you ring a real one.

Practise It First

Your co-founder is about to write the words with you, then play a business owner and let you get it wrong a few times, where it does not cost you anything.
