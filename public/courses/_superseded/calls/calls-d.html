Why it worked

Five things happened in that call

He opened with something true and specific. Not "do you need a receptionist". He rang at eight and nobody answered. That is a fact about Dave's business, and Dave knows it is true.

Dave described his own problem. "Bit of a nightmare honestly." The moment they say that, you have stopped selling and started helping.

The price question got parked, not dodged. "Give me two minutes or I'll quote you for the wrong thing." Reasonable, not evasive.

Dave said the numbers. Five or six missed. A couple hundred a callout. Sam only repeated Dave's own maths back to him.

The ask was a choice of two. Not "when are you free". Thursday morning or Thursday afternoon. People answer that one without thinking.

The bit to steal

Two sentences worth memorising

"Give me two minutes to understand what you actually need, or I'll quote you for the wrong thing."

"Are you around Thursday morning, or is the afternoon better?"

Those two get used in every call you ever make. The rest is just being a person.

What happens in the meeting

You have booked the time. The meeting is a completely different conversation, and a much easier one.
